# CISLR Dataset Card (VoiceAlly)

**Status: AUDITED; SUPERVISED PIPELINE NOT FEASIBLE FOR THE LOCAL SUBSET.**
`src/data/audit_dataset.py` has been run against the real, uploaded CISLR
data. Per project rules, nothing here may be filled with an assumed or
estimated number.

## Source
- Dataset: CISLR (Indian Sign Language)
- Location: https://huggingface.co/datasets/Exploration-Lab/CISLR
- Annotations supplied locally on: 2026-09-22

## Current supplied delivery (2026-09-22)
- MP4 files: 7,050 (under `data/raw/CISLR_v1.5-a_videos/`).
- Labels: `dataset.csv` maps `uid` to `gloss`, duration, and category.
- Derived manifest: `data/raw/local_manifest.csv`; all 7,050 labels map to
  an existing MP4. Two duplicate-content groups were detected by SHA-256.
- Vocabulary: 4,764 glosses; 1--13 videos per gloss (median 1).
- Official partitions: 4,765 prototypes and 2,285 tests.
- Signer identifiers: unavailable. No filename-derived identity is used.

## Statistics
Measured in `results/tables/dataset_statistics.csv`: 7,050 videos, 4,764
classes, 1--13 videos/class, no missing files and two duplicate-content
groups,
median annotated duration 6 seconds, and p95 duration 13 seconds.

## Signer metadata availability
Unavailable. The supplied annotations have no signer field. The primary
signer-independent experiment is therefore not valid and has not been run.

## Vocabulary selection rule
Rule (fixed in code, `src/data/select_vocabulary.py`): a class is
selected iff it has at least `min_videos_per_class` usable videos AND
(if signer metadata exists) at least `min_signers_per_class` distinct
signers. Default thresholds (15 videos, 3 signers) are starting points
to be revisited once real per-class counts are known -- see the CLI
flags in that script.

The predefined 15-video threshold selected 0/4,764 classes; the largest
class has 13 videos. See `data/processed/vocabulary.csv`. Reducing this
threshold would not make a train/validation/test protocol credible.

## Known limitations
This is a sparse, official prototype/test corpus rather than a sufficiently
sampled supervised recognition benchmark. It may support a separately
designed one-shot/prototype experiment, but not the requested controlled
MLP/LSTM/GRU/TCN training comparison.
